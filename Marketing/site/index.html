How it works

You already know the controls.

No buttons, no pedals, no tutorial. Your thumb never leaves the glass.

Hold to go left

Press and hold anywhere on the screen. The car banks left for as long as you hold.

Release to go right

Let go and the car drifts back to the right. Tapping in rhythm is how you thread the corners.

Stay on the road

Touch either edge and the run is over. Your distance and your best score are all that matter.

The road wins eventually

Every run gets harder than the last.

The road tightens every 300 meters. It speeds up, the lane narrows, and past a certain point it starts throwing in extra curves just to see you off.

  • Distance How far this run has lasted, in meters.
  • Level Climbs every 300 m — faster, then narrower, then both.
  • Best Your furthest run, saved on the device.
Pick your fight

Three difficulties. Start where you like.

Easy begins on level 1. Medium drops you in at level 5. Hard starts at level 10 — near top speed, on a road that's already narrow. Your call.

  • Easy The full climb from a standing start.
  • Medium Skip the warm-up.
  • Hard Straight into the deep end.
