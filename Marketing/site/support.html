Support

Stay On for iPhone

Something not working, or a question the FAQ doesn't answer? Email support@stayondriving.com and include your device model and iOS version — it helps a lot.

How do I play?

Hold anywhere on the screen to steer left. Release to steer right. Stay between the two glowing edges of the road for as long as you can. Touching an edge ends the run.

The game feels too hard / too easy

On the start screen and after a crash, pick Easy, Medium, or Hard. Easy starts from the beginning; Medium and Hard drop you further up the difficulty ladder.

How do I turn the music off?

Tap the speaker icon in the bottom-right corner. Your choice is remembered.

I bought "Remove Ads" but ads are still showing

Open the crash screen and tap Restore purchase. Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple Account you used to buy it. If it still doesn't restore, email us with the date of purchase and we'll sort it out.

I was charged but nothing unlocked

In-App Purchases are handled by Apple. Try Restore purchase first. For refunds or billing issues, use Apple's Report a Problem page. We can also look into it if you email us.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once installed, Stay On needs no connection to play.

Is my score saved if I get a new phone?

Best distance is stored locally on the device, so it does not transfer automatically. There is no account to sign in to.

Privacy

See the Privacy Policy for what the app collects. Short version: no account, no personal information, anonymous analytics only.
