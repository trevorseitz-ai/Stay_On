Launch decision to make first: ship iPhone/iPad, Android, or both? The fastest path is normally to finish one platform first, but build the shared game and store assets so the second platform is mostly packaging and QA.
Additional suggestions before launch
  • Do not spend time adding more driving mechanics until a small test group confirms that the first-session loop is fun and understandable.
  • Decide whether billboards are decorative MVP scenery or real ad inventory. Real ads add SDK, consent, privacy, testing, and review work.
  • Prepare a simple support page, privacy policy, and “report a problem” email before submission; they make review and launch support easier.
  • Use a staged rollout and keep the web version live as a fallback while the store builds are reviewed.
